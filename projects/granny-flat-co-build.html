DESIGN / 01

Granny Flat Co-Build

WORK
Collaborative residential construction
YEAR
2023
MEDIUM
Residential construction, timber framing, cladding & masonry
CONTEXT
Employed with A&J Creative Industries Pty Ltd · Co-built with my father

Overview

During my gap year, I spent approximately ten months helping build a boutique granny flat from the ground up while employed with A&J Creative Industries Pty Ltd. The core build was primarily a collaboration between my father and me, with specialist tradespeople involved at different stages.

The design brings distinctive details into a compact home, including curved ceiling forms, a pitched bedroom ceiling, recycled fence-post cladding, contemporary strip lighting and a stone feature wall that I constructed.

Design Brief

My role was to help turn an existing set of building plans and A&J Creative Industries’ design and management decisions into a finished, modern living space. The work needed to follow applicable Australian building and safety requirements while coordinating materials, construction stages and the involvement of qualified trades.

I was not the sole designer or builder. I worked alongside my father and the wider project team, taking responsibility for particular hands-on elements within the overall construction.

Process

Working through the build gave me experience across a broad construction sequence, from framing and concreting through to cladding, insulation, plastering, roofing, interior fit-out and landscaping. I took direct responsibility for selected room-framing work, the recycled timber interior cladding and the masonry rock wall, while collaborating on many other parts of the build.

The project required regular coordination with experienced tradespeople, including electricians and plumbers. Observing their work and understanding how each stage depended on the previous one was as valuable as learning to use tools and materials myself. I also developed a better understanding of construction deadlines, safe working practices and the importance of preparing an area so the next trade could work efficiently.

Particular attention went into the distinctive interior details, where reclaimed materials and unusual ceiling forms had to sit comfortably alongside the more conventional elements of a residential building.

Outcome

The experience gave me a much broader practical understanding of how a building comes together. I gained confidence with framing, masonry, cladding and general site work, and became more familiar with the responsibilities and methods of the specialist trades involved.

I am especially proud of the parts I personally helped shape, including the recycled timber work and the stone feature wall. Just as importantly, the project taught me how to contribute reliably within a team, respond to other people’s requirements and bring an ambitious design through a long, coordinated build.
